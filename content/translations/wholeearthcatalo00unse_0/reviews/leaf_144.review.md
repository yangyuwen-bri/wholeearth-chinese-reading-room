# Leaf 144 Independent Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Coverage Evidence

- Source inventory: 121 independent Book People mail-order listings spanning P, R, S, T, U, V, W, and X/Y/Z; every listing includes a title, price, and all printed seasonal issue codes; one cartoon speech balloon and one common order address.
- Translation coverage: every listing was transcribed in visual column order, translated individually, retained with its English title, and checked for issue-code and price accuracy against n144 at 2000 pixels.
- Permitted omissions: none. The large baby/globe photograph and uncaptioned vendor cartoon add no further prose beyond the translated speech balloon.

## Reasons

- No catalog line is collapsed into a subject or price summary; four Step by Step titles, two Space Enclosure volumes, and two Tensile Structure volumes remain separate records.
- A line-count check confirms P 20, R 4, S 49, T 20, U 5, V 5, W 11, and X/Y/Z 7 entries.
- Prices retain cents exactly, including $15.05, $16.65, $0.95, and $1.00; issue codes are preserved for every title where printed.
- The anomalous `F 96` on `Year from Monday` was visually verified as a source printing and explicitly marked rather than silently corrected.

## Required Fixes

- 无。

## Residual Risks

- A few book titles use historically idiosyncratic punctuation or missing function words; English originals are retained beside the Chinese rendering for exact retrieval.
- Seasonal codes and prices are historical catalog data and should not be interpreted as current availability.
