FUNCTION
The WHOLE EARTH CATALOG functions as an evaluation and access
device. With it, the user should know better what is worth getting and
where and how to do the getting.
An item is listed in the CATALOG if it is deemed:
1) Useful as a tool,
2) Relevant to independent education,
3) High quality or low cost,
4) Easily available by mail.
CATALOG listings are continually revised according to the experience
and suggestions of CATALOG users and staff.
PURPOSE
We are as gods and might as well get good at it. So far remotely done
power and glory——as via government, big business, formal education,
church——has succeeded to point where gross defects obscure actual
gains. In response to this dilemma and to these gains a realm of intimate,
personal power is developing——power of the individual to conduct his
own education, find his own inspiration, shape his own environment, and
share his adventure with whoever
process are sought and promoted by the WHOLE EARTH CATALOG.

## Final Translation

## 功能

《全球概览》是一种评价和获取工具。有了它，使用者应当能更清楚地知道：
什么值得取得，以及从哪里、用什么办法取得。

一项物品若被认为符合以下条件，便会收入《概览》：

1. 作为工具有用；
2. 与自主教育有关；
3. 品质优良或价格低廉；
4. 很容易通过邮购获得。

《概览》中的条目会根据使用者和工作人员的经验与建议不断修订。

## 宗旨

我们宛如众神，最好把这件事做好。迄今为止，由政府、大企业、正规教育、
教会等远程施行的权力与荣耀，已经发展到这样一个地步：严重的缺陷遮蔽了
真正的收益。为了回应这种困境，也为了回应这些收益，一种亲近的、个人的
权力领域正在形成——个人可以主持自己的教育，找到自己的灵感，塑造自己
的环境，并同任何感兴趣的人分享他的探索。凡是有助于这一过程的工具，
《全球概览》都会寻找并推广。
