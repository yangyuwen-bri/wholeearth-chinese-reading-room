# Leaf 014 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- The two source records remain distinct. The Dover review, free-catalog
  address, handling charge, prepaid-order postage threshold, reproduced
  catalog cover, four-column listing, and `"Teach Yourself" Series` are not
  mixed with the separate `Good News` review, order block, cover, and signed
  excerpt.
- The translation retains all 70 visible Dover bullets: 60 entries in the
  four-column general listing and 10 opening titles in the `"Teach Yourself"`
  series. Direct comparison with the 600-dpi rendering of PDF page 15 confirms
  that the individual prices and the visible multi-volume prices are assigned
  to the correct records.
- In particular, the paired volume and set prices for `The Art of
  Organ-Building`, `Theory of Ship Motions`, `Music of the Spheres`, and `The
  Principles of Psychology` are correct. The set-only records for the Thoreau
  journal and the Catlin volumes are also retained correctly.
- The entries omitted by the official OCR but legible in the scan, including
  `Algebra`, `Amateur Acting`, the `Soap Bubbles` price, and the `The Indians'
  Book` price, have been recovered accurately. The obscured price after `On
  the Sensations of Tone` has not been guessed.
- The `Good News` material is complete: the editorial recommendation,
  publication rhythm, 15-cent price, address, Fuller reference, sphere model,
  least-work principle, marble-cake comparison, changing perimeter,
  convergence of specialties, authors, article title, and periodical credit
  are all present without summary reduction.
- Regression review confirms that the Powell format is now transcribed as
  `5⅝ × 8⅜`, the Bagnold asterisk is retained and identified as unexplained in
  the source, and the omission, OCR, and self-critique sections have been
  updated consistently. No required fidelity defect remains.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- The source reproduction after the Helmholtz format
  (`6⅛ × 9¼`) is smeared; no price can be read reliably from the 600-dpi page.
  Leaving that price unstated remains preferable to reconstruction from an
  external edition.
- Codes such as `T Pa`, `S Pa`, `T Cl`, `S Cl`, `USO`, `USCO`, `EBE`, `EBEC`,
  `NYP`, and `Tent.` have no legend on this leaf. Preserving them without
  expansion is appropriate.
- `Dover Publications`, `Good News`, `circle of enlightenment`, and the two
  consciousness terms are proposed leaf-level renderings rather than accepted
  shared-glossary entries; issue-wide promotion remains an editorial decision.
