## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- The translation retains all four visible textual elements on the cover:
  `WHOLE EARTH CATALOG`, `access to tools`, `Spring 1969`, and `$4`.
- The established publication title `《全球概览》` and the provisional phrase
  `获取工具` are used consistently with the issue glossary.
- The season/year and price remain distinct lines and preserve their original
  meaning; the `$4` price is not silently discarded as routine metadata.
- Direct comparison with the official OCR JSON and the high-resolution
  rendering of PDF page 1 confirms that the scan supplies the title, tagline,
  and price omitted by OCR.
- The reader-facing translation contains no page-description language,
  evidence-quality workflow language, invented image caption, or external
  background fact.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- `access to tools` remains `provisional` in the shared glossary. The current
  rendering as `获取工具` is faithful here, but the orchestrator may still
  stabilize the phrase issue-wide.
- Official OCR captures only `Spring 1969`; acceptance of the other three
  elements therefore depends on the visually checked high-resolution scan.
