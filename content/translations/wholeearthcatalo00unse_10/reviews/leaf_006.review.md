# Leaf 006 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- Direct comparison with the 300-dpi rendering of PDF page 7 and the source
  pack found no substantive omission or record crossover. The three records
  for `The Population Bomb`, `Famine 1975!`, and
  `The Times Atlas of the World` remain separate.
- `The Population Bomb` retains the review's deliberately harsh opening,
  each long quotation, the population-control and environmental arguments,
  the ship satire, the DDT passage, the Los Angeles smog line, and the complete
  author/year/page/price/order record.
- `Famine 1975!` retains the review, the cover subtitle, both quoted passages,
  the `12,000`-deaths statement, and all seven country classifications without
  smoothing over `Can't-be-saved`, `Walking Wounded`, or
  `Should Receive Food`. Its author/year/page/price/order record is complete.
- The atlas review retains the Northcliffe dictum, John Bartholomew credit,
  physical dimensions, eleven-pound weight, 568-page total, 240 map pages,
  more than 200,000 index entries, naming rule, dedication, binding, the
  `$45` prose price, reviewer credit, and the distinct `$57.50 postpaid`
  product price and order address.
- Both bottom captions, `Distribution and Density of Population` and
  `Birth and death rates`, are restored from the scan despite incomplete OCR.
  The scan-supported corrections to Ehrlich's name and the `12½"` dimension
  are also recorded accurately.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- The three Chinese book titles and `carrying capacity` are explicitly
  provisional and still require issue-wide glossary stabilization.
- Tiny labels internal to the reproduced book covers and atlas maps are not
  stable enough to transcribe. The translation correctly avoids guessing them.
- The source itself gives `$45` in the atlas review and `$57.50 postpaid` in
  the product record. Both should remain; later editing should not normalize
  one to the other.
