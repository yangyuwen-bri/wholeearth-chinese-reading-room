# Leaf 130 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- After rotating the local calibration image 90 degrees clockwise, the three
  supplier-policy paragraphs are fully legible and remain three distinct
  paragraphs in the translation. The translation retains the lowest-cost
  direct-access purpose, supplier addresses and prices, the no-commission
  policy, purchasable advertising and rate inquiry, the editorial basis for
  listings, possible supplier price advantage, the non-purchasable listing
  service, the absence of manufacturer or supplier listing charges, and the
  request for order quantities received through the forms.
- The address panel reads `Portola Institute, Inc.`, `558 Santa Cruz`,
  `Menlo Park, CA 94025`, and `(415) 323-0313`; every element is retained
  accurately in the translation.
- The oppositely oriented form labels read `Supplier` and `zip` when that
  region is rotated 180 degrees. `供应商` and `邮政编码` preserve both field
  meanings without guessing additional form text.
- The seven Catalog categories are all present and correctly mapped:
  `Understanding Whole Systems`, `Shelter and Land Use`,
  `Industry and Craft`, `Communications`, `Community`, `Nomadics`, and
  `Learning`.
- `The WHOLE EARTH CATALOG is published in the Spring and Fall.` is faithfully
  retained as `《全球概览》在春季和秋季出版。`
- The orientation notes are accurate: the stored image is counter-clockwise
  relative to the main text and becomes upright after a 90-degree clockwise
  rotation, while the `Supplier` and `zip` labels then remain upside down and
  require a separate 180-degree rotation to read upright.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- The official OCR is heavily degraded by the rotated layout, especially for
  the address and form fields. The local calibration image resolves the
  reviewed items clearly, so this does not block acceptance.
