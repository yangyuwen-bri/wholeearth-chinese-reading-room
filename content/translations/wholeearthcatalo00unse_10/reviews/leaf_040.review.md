## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- The 600 dpi rendering of PDF page 41 (`n40`) confirms seven `Sundry
  Materials` groups: ferro-cement, membranes, hubs, plastics, dome-seam tapes,
  foam, and Laminite cardboard. The translation preserves those boundaries and
  does not move a supplier, price, brochure, label, or photograph into the
  wrong group.
- The ferro-cement section retains the `3/4 in` construction claim, Pier Luigi
  Nervi passage, `$1.00` National Fisherman reprint, the Jackson/Sutherland
  book record, `$5-7` handbooks and Saga address, and the September 1968
  `Popular Science` furniture caption.
- The complete Windboats/Seacrete panel agrees with the scan: Lloyd's
  classification `100 A.1.`, confirmation date `7/12/65`, Windboats name and
  `NOR 03 Z` address ending, density `151` with `36/100` comparisons, and the
  two distinct tensile-test groups. The centered-load panel is correctly
  `48 x 12 x 7/8 in` with `1900/3600/5340 psi`, while the separate tensile
  stress values are `1300/1690 psi`.
- The Seacrete compression data retain the `6 x 6 x 6 in` specimen, `7/14/28`
  day columns, `116/135.5/196.5` ton failing loads, and
  `7217.3/8742.2/12225 psi` ultimate stresses without row or column drift. The
  clipped `Young's Modulus` heading is preserved without invented values.
- Every membrane source remains correctly associated: clear/aluminized Mylar,
  clear vinyl at about `6 cents/sq. ft.`, both coated-fabric brochures and
  addresses, the `24 ft` parachute at `$22.50` plus postage and tax, and the two
  coated-nylon-fabric brochures from West Point Pepperell.
- The hub and plastics groups retain `$1 per strut`, Dyna-Strux and the
  `DYNA DOME` image text; Plasticraft's complete catalog; Abe Schuster's clear
  acrylic factory seconds and bargaining note; and the Tedlar-coated
  fiberglass-panel supplier. All visible addresses match the page.
- The dome-seam-tape group is complete. Fab-Dek keeps `35 mil`, `3 in`,
  `11 cents/linear ft`, `$4.50/gal`, F.O.B. plant, and both Miracle addresses.
  Its reproduced label retains one-sheet roofing, Hypalon, asbestos felt,
  Neoprene, all four resistance claims, all four installation advantages,
  the crack-bridging guarantee, company line, and the `HYPALON`/`KOLORPRENE`
  copyright-name statements. The separate neoprene tape, isopthalic-resin
  tape, and 3M pressure-sensitive-tape brochure keep their own specifications
  and suppliers.
- The foam and Laminite blocks retain the full brochure titles, Mobay and
  Sealzit/Flintkote contacts, local yellow-pages instruction, Laminite's three
  material claims, and the complete sample address.
- The file contains the required seven top-level sections in the standard
  order. Source-pack mapping, `906` OCR words in `147` lines, source-pack-gap
  disclosure, uncertainty notes, and historical-claims framing are internally
  consistent.

## Residual Risks

- The ferro-cement furniture photograph reproduces an additional tiny caption
  inside the photographed magazine clipping. Its halftone and size prevent a
  reliable character-by-character reading even at 600 dpi. The Catalog's own
  stable caption is complete, and no reconstruction should be imported from an
  external issue of `Popular Science`.
- The source itself cuts off the Seacrete sheet immediately after the
  `(d) YOUNG'S MODULUS` heading. No modulus value can be inferred from this
  leaf.
- `NOR 03 Z`, `isopthalic`, and the two `copyright name` statements are
  historical printed forms. Preserving them with explicit uncertainty or
  source-form notes is preferable to silent modernization.
- Fire resistance, weather resistance, structural strength, insulation, and
  durability statements are historical supplier or publication claims, not
  current certification.
