# Leaf 001 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- `FUNCTION` is translated as a complete argument rather than a summary. It
  retains the Catalog's paired evaluation/access role, what/where/how framing,
  all four numbered listing criteria, and continual revision from user and
  staff experience and suggestions.
- `PURPOSE` retains the opening claim, the criticism of remotely exercised
  power through government, big business, formal education, and church, the
  tension between defects and actual gains, the emerging realm of intimate
  personal power, all four individual capacities, the sharing of one's
  adventure, and the Catalog's search for and promotion of enabling tools.
- All eight retaining subscribers remain separate records, with the correct
  scan-confirmed name/location associations:
  Mark Albert/San Francisco; John Doss/San Francisco; Edmund Scientific
  Company/Barrington; Henry Jacobs & Associates/Sausalito; Edward
  Rosenfeld/New York; St. George Bryan/Maui; Geoffrey Gates/New York; and
  Peter Cornell/Berkeley.
- `Second Printing August 1969`, the Portola Institute copyright line, and the
  Pan-American and International copyright-conventions notice are all
  retained.
- The attached mailing-flap labels `FROM` and `PLACE STAMP HERE` remain
  distinct from the editorial text and are translated as form instructions.
- Direct comparison with the official OCR JSON and the high-resolution
  rendering of PDF page 2 found no substantive omission, list mismatch,
  invented background fact, summary drift, or reader-facing workflow leakage.

- None.

## Residual Risks

- `retaining subscriber` is rendered as `支持订户` and explicitly marked
  provisional in this leaf's glossary updates. It should be stabilized against
  the subscription tiers on leaf 003 before issue-wide glossary acceptance.
- The opening sentence `We are as gods and might as well get good at it` has
  several viable Chinese renderings. `我们如同神明，不妨把这件事做得更好一些`
  preserves the claim and exhortation, but the orchestrator may prefer a
  project-wide canonical wording if the sentence recurs.
