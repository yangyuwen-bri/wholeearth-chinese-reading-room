# Leaf 002 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- Direct comparison with the calibration image and a 300-dpi rendering of
  local PDF page 3 confirms the four-column reading order. The translation
  follows the printed sequence column by column: p.1–32, p.33–61, p.62–96,
  and p.97–129. The three column transitions preserve the source's p.32/33,
  p.61/62, and p.96/97 continuations.
- The page anchors form one complete, nonduplicated sequence from p.1 through
  p.129. The two printed spans are correctly retained as p.3–4 for
  `Buckminster Fuller` and p.64–65 for `Mathematics`; no single-page anchor is
  skipped or assigned to an adjacent entry.
- All seven printed section boundaries and their contents-page ranges remain
  distinct: `Understanding Whole Systems` p.3–17, `Shelter and Land Use`
  p.18–39, `Industry and Craft` p.40–59, `Communications` p.60–77,
  `Community` p.78–93, `Nomadics` p.94–107, and `Learning` p.108–125.
- Every visible article, book, periodical, supplier, product, tool, and name
  was checked individually in its printed top-to-bottom order. Multi-entry
  page groups remain separate rather than being collapsed into descriptions;
  this includes the four entries on p.7, p.33, p.52, p.54, p.59, p.101,
  p.103, and p.114, and the five entries on p.113.
- The `Shelter and Land Use` credit `Edited by Lloyd Kahn` is retained as
  `劳埃德·卡恩（Lloyd Kahn）编辑` and is not mistaken for a directory entry.
- Scan-sensitive or easily normalized source forms remain recoverable in the
  translation: `Designcourse`, `CUM Yarn Samples`, `Colour Guitar`,
  `9100 A Calculator`, `The Explorers Trademark Log`, `Piaget?`, `Krazy Leg`,
  `WFF ‘N PROOF`, and `ITA` preserve their original English spelling or
  punctuation.
- The scan-confirmed p.107 entry `Nothing` is explicitly translated as
  `什么也没有（Nothing）`; it is not treated as an OCR gap. The final four
  anchors also match the page exactly: `Advertisements` p.126, `CATALOG
  costs` and `Whole Earth Truck Store` p.127, `Subscription forms` p.128,
  and `Portola Institute` p.129.
- The reader-facing translation contains no prose summary of the directory,
  invented background facts, or evidence-quality workflow language. The
  source pack confines scan and OCR discussion to its notes.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- The seven Chinese section labels remain provisional in the shared glossary.
  Their English originals and exact boundaries are present here, so this does
  not block fidelity acceptance, but issue-wide wording may still be
  stabilized by the orchestrator.
- `Coot` and `Boffers` are too context-poor on the contents page to identify
  securely. The current translation appropriately keeps `Coot` and `Boffers`
  visible beside cautious Chinese renderings; later body pages may support
  more specific issue-wide names.
- The very large page heading `CONTENTS` and the p.1 directory entry
  `CONTENTS` repeat the same source word. The translation normalizes that
  repetition into the p.1 `目录（CONTENTS）` entry rather than printing a
  second identical Chinese heading; no distinct navigational information is
  lost.
- Official DjVu OCR interleaves large section labels with entry lines and is
  not reliable evidence for column hierarchy. Acceptance therefore depends on
  the independently checked calibration image and local PDF rendering.
