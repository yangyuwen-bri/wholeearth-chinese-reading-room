## Residual Risks

- `booklime` has been used for more than one water-loving plant, while this
  page supplies no Latin name. A species-level Chinese identification would be
  an unsupported addition; retaining the English common name is safer if the
  generic Chinese term cannot be established from the issue's own evidence.
- The tiny anatomical letters inside the bee-stinger drawing and the unlabeled
  brood-photo grid are visual details, not additional Catalog prose. At 600
  dpi the stable textual evidence is the `Bee stinger magnified` caption; the
  draft appropriately avoids inventing explanations for the small labels.
- The hive-handling excerpt genuinely ends at `More par-` on this page. Later
  regression must preserve the explicit break rather than borrowing text from
  another edition of the bee book.
- The historical recipes and plant-use claims include material that would
  require modern safety qualification if republished as advice. Their presence
  here is acceptable only as faithful translation of the 1969 page, with the
  existing evidence/safety framing retained.
