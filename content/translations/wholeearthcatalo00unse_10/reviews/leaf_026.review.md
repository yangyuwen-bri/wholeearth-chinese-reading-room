  International Development, State Department, Washington, D.C.

  Water-Power Sites`, `How to Salt Fish`, `Making Building Blocks with

## Residual Risks

- A few tiny stove-section dimensions remain degraded even at 600 dpi. They
  should remain explicitly uncertain rather than be inferred from a modern
  stove plan.
- The handwritten-looking `2 HOSE` label is awkward, but two hose runs are
  visible. The existing uncertainty note appropriately distinguishes the
  visible configuration from an unspecified hose size.
- The catalog's “plans, rent, or sell” statement applies to catalog-listed
  items generally; the 54-item future-supplement list has a different
  development status. That distinction should remain explicit after revision.
