  Chardin's `The Phenomenon of Man`. Their reviews, excerpts, figures,
  bibliographic blocks, prices, suppliers, and contributor credit have been
  assigned to the correct entries.

  population growth despite epidemics and war; the India birth-control
  episode; avalanche rather than explosion; lead paint; and nutrition,
  transport, and declining infectious-disease mortality.
- Teilhard's Catalog commentary, the plan and aim quotations, energy passage,

  minimum estimates and the convergence region is not to scale. Abbreviations
  such as `Sinanthr.`, `Pithecanthr.`, and `Australopith.` have correctly not
  been expanded.

  Teilhard address exactly as printed: `49 East 33 Street`. The credit
  `[Reviewed by Dave Evans. Suggested by Julia Brand.]` is also retained.

  recoverable English anchor at first occurrence. The two occurrences of the
  source's deliberately capitalized `the All` and `the Person` now consistently
  read “整体”（the All） and “个人”（the Person）. All three terms are recorded
  in the leaf's terminology decisions and the shared issue glossary.
