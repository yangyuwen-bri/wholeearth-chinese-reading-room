## Reasons

- The two independent records remain distinct. `Notes on the Synthesis of
  Form` retains the Catalog review, table-of-contents extract, complete
  bibliographic/order block, legible cover title and author, and all of the
  substantive excerpts distributed around the cover and network diagrams.
  `On Growth and Form` begins only after the visual divider and is not mixed
  with Alexander's material.

  negative formulation through misfits, the continuing adjustment made by a
  form's inhabitants, the two circled subsystems and their independent
  adjustment, the contrast with the selfconscious situation, response to
  change, Mousgoum maintenance, conceptual bias, unified description through

  problem.

  order around the two network diagrams. The pale continuation below them

  `$6.75` postpaid, Harvard University Press, `79 Garden Street`, `Cambridge,
