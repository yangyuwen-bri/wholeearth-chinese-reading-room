## Residual Risks

- Three small experiment photographs in the lower middle of the first record
  have severely faded, halftoned micro-captions. Even targeted high-resolution
  inspection supports only fragments such as `Figure 6-10` and `Figure 6-11`,
  not a stable character-by-character transcription. The translation correctly
  discloses this instead of inventing captions.
- The source itself contains the anomalous board-foot parenthetical and the
  apparent `slide`/`splice` distinction in Figure F-2. These printed forms have
  been preserved and flagged; a silent technical correction would exceed the
  evidence on the page.
- Performance, durability, market, construction, and material-safety claims
  reflect a 1966-1969 report and supplier context. They should not be treated
  as current structural certification or present-day handling guidance.
