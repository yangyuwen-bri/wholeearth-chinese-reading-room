# Leaf 019 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- The page is correctly divided into three independent records: `Space Grid
  Structures`, `The Dymaxion World of Buckminster Fuller`, and `Fuller Sun
  Dome`. Their reviews, images, figures, bibliographic blocks, prices, supplier
  addresses, and credits have not been merged.
- The `Space Grid Structures` account preserves the Octetruss date and
  dimensions (`1959`, `35'`, `60'`, `40'`, and `2"`), the two-plane and web
  member description, the three book sections, the 1968/200-page record,
  `$12.50`, and the MIT Press address.
- Plate 109's substantive engineering details are complete: Dunstable,
  Arthur Farebrother and Partners, Space Structures Research, Ltd., Z. S.
  Makowski, 12 segments, 64 elongated tetrahedra per segment, and the Plate
  110 cross-reference. The architectural term `reflected ceiling plan` is now
  accurately rendered as “天花反射平面图（reflected ceiling plan）”.
- The Dymaxion record retains Robert W. Marks, 1960, 232 pages, `$12.50`, the
  Southern Illinois University Press order block, patent Figures 15, 16, and
  18, Richard Buckminster Fuller as inventor, and the `Dymaxion car 1933`
  plan and side views.
- The Sun Dome review retains the `$5` construction license, specified and
  alternative materials, two-triangle system, approximately `35'` maximum
  diameter, 1966 plan record, complete Popular Science Monthly address, Ken
  Babbs credit, construction-license certificate title, and Lloyd Kahn
  photograph credit.
- At 600 dpi, the reproduced Sun Dome instruction sheet has four legible
  numbered step headings in addition to its main title. The revised draft
  retains all four headings in Chinese with their source-language forms, while
  appropriately declining to reconstruct the degraded smaller body text.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- Most body text inside the reproduced instruction sheet and construction
  license remains too small or degraded for dependable transcription even at
  600 dpi. The draft is right not to reconstruct it.
- The dense point letters and dimensions within patent Figures 15, 16, and 18
  have no separate explanatory prose. Preserving the figure numbers, inventor,
  and adjacent car caption without inventing meanings is appropriate.
