# Leaf 032 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- The review compared `leaf_032.md` with the official OCR source pack and a
  600 dpi rendering of PDF page 33 (`n32`). The page clearly contains three
  records: `Farmers of Forty Centuries`, `How to Have a Green Thumb Without
  an Aching Back`, and the single `Soil Test Kit` record spanning the lower
  center and right. The draft preserves those boundaries correctly.
- Wendell Berry's full review is present in source order. It retains the
  dictionary definitions, the invader/colonizer argument, public patriotism
  versus private land-use behavior, the need for models, the American Indian
  and other indigenous examples, King's 1907 journey, the return of organic
  matter, farming methods, travel-book comparison, photographs, and the final
  land/mind contrast. No whole paragraph is missing or reduced to a summary.
- Berry's polemical force is preserved, and the earlier defective rendering
  of `worked back into the soil` is now accurately expressed as
  “重新施回土壤中.”
- The two order blocks match the scan:
  `F. H. King / 1811 / 441 pp. / $5.00 / Hillary House Publishers, Ltd. /
  303 Park Avenue South / New York 10010` and
  `Ruth Stout / 1955 / 160 pp. (dk. green text) / $1.45 /
  Cornerstone Library Publications / Simon & Schuster Inc. /
  630 Fifth Avenue / New York 10020`.
- The visibly printed `1811` has been restored in the bibliography and
  self-check tuple. The OCR note now explicitly identifies it as a probable
  source typographical error instead of silently substituting `1911`.
- The soil-test record retains acidity/alkalinity, nitrogen, phosphorus,
  potash, the Organic Supplement, the four nutrient/amendment paragraphs,
  both product models, the supplier, ZIP code, and full price range. The
  following scan values are correct in the draft:
  `8 inches`, `Model H $8.95`, `75 flowers`, `50 vegetables`,
  `Model B $20.95`, `DELUXE $39.95`, `225 flowers/vegetables`,
  `01776`, and `kits $5.95-$39.95`. Model H now says it can perform
  `50` individual tests covering nitrogen, phosphorus, potash, and acidity,
  without implying 50 tests for each measure.
- The file contains the required seven top-level sections in the expected
  order: `Source Pack`, `Context Notes`, `Glossary Updates`,
  `Final Translation`, `Omitted Bibliographic/Order Info`,
  `OCR / Uncertainty Notes`, and `Self Critique`. Source-pack leaf/PDF
  mapping, printed page, URLs, OCR counts, and null-risk disclosure match
  `leaf_032.json`.

## Required Fixes

- None.

## Residual Risks

- `1811` is very likely an error in the 1969 Catalog's order block, but it is
  visibly printed that way. Any historically corrected year should be added
  only as an explicit editorial note, not substituted for the source.
- The source page itself has no visible running section heading. The Source
  Pack's `Shelter and Land Use` label is plausible as the Catalog's parent
  section, while the preceding page visibly uses the narrower `Organic
  Gardening` subsection; this hierarchy cannot be settled from `n32` alone
  and does not affect the three record boundaries.
- The reproduced book-spread photographs contain type too small for reliable
  line-by-line recovery even at 600 dpi. They are illustrations of King's
  book, not additional Catalog records; the legible Catalog review surrounding
  them is complete.
