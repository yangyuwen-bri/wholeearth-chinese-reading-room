accepted

## Reasons

- The two records are correctly separated. Ken Kern's review, excerpts, and
  diagrams remain under `The Owner-Built Home`, while Rex Roberts's review,
  door/window material, seasonal-sun material, and wall section remain under
  `Your Engineered House`.
- Both order blocks are accurate: Kern is `1961; 300 pp.`, `$10.00 postpaid`,
  Ken Kern Drafting, Sierra Route, Oakhurst, CA `93644`; Roberts is `1964;
  237 pp.`, `$7.50 postpaid`, J. B. Lippincott Company, East Washington
  Square, Philadelphia, Pa. `19105`, or WHOLE EARTH CATALOG. Kern's cover
  price of two dollars per volume is also correctly distinguished from the
  ten-dollar book-and-design offer.
- The Rikyū passage is faithful in sequence and meaning: Sakai, the obstructed
  sea view, the water basin, the glimpse through the trees, and the relation
  between the dipperful, the ocean, the guest's place in the universe, and the
  infinite are all retained.
- The glare, exterior-shade, passive-ventilation, seasonal-sun, refrigerator-
  type door, separate fixed-window/ventilator, handed-lighting, and wall-layer
  explanations are substantially faithful. The Roberts wall labels are
  correctly assigned and the two shiny-face directions of the aluminum foil
  are not reversed.
- Regression review confirms that the fireplace now distinguishes the `28"`
  hearth from the firebox and retains `tile over` and `adobe wall`.
- The plumbing diagram now retains the `2" wet vent`, assigns the three `1½"`
  traps to the kitchen sink, lavatory, and bath, and correctly transcribes the
  floor fitting as `3 x 1½ tapped sanitary tee`.
- The roller-supported-form account now preserves `8"` plain rammed earth,
  `4"` soil-cement plating, the `1¼"` pipe, `¾"` plywood sides and bolt, the
  `36"` span, `28½"` end-view dimension, and `24"` layers. It no longer turns
  the layer dimension into a count.
- The CBRI light-duty floor, conventional concrete floor, wood floor, and
  hardwood-over-concrete detail are now separately attributed. Wire mesh is
  no longer assigned to the CBRI system, the unsupported `2×6` joist has been
  removed, and the `1×2` sleepers remain with the correct detail.
- The arid-region figure now retains the `40°` marker. The uncertainty notes
  and self-critique also reflect the corrected transcriptions and no longer
  repeat the superseded values.

## Residual Risks

- A few handwritten fireplace, plumbing, form, and floor labels remain
  degraded by the original reproduction. The draft appropriately limits its
  technical account to labels already resolved in the high-resolution review
  rather than normalizing unresolved marks from modern construction practice.
- The seasonal-sun drawing supplies directional labels rather than a numerical
  winter/summer angle. The translation is right not to invent a degree value
  for that Roberts diagram.
