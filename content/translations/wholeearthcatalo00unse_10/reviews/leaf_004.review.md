# Leaf 004 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- Direct comparison with the 300-dpi rendering of PDF page 5 and the official
  OCR JSON found no substantive omission in the source prose. The translation
  retains the `Whole Systems` heading, the complete Fuller assessment, all six
  named books, the long `No More Secondhand God` verse, and the visible
  signatures and attributions.
- The three complete bibliographic/order records remain correctly separated:
  `Operating Manual for Spaceship Earth` is attached to the lever and
  chick/hatching passages and six short statements; `Ideas and Integrities` is
  attached to the lake passage and the synergy definition; `No More Secondhand
  God` is attached to the selfishness and thinking definitions. Their authors,
  years, page counts, prices, publishers, addresses, and Whole Earth Catalog
  alternatives are all retained without crossing records.
- The lower-gutter continuation is correctly reconstructed. `continued`
  connects the two phantom-captain blocks, and the complete passage is
  attributed to `Nine Chains to the Moon`, not to the neighboring
  `No More Secondhand God` excerpts.
- The draft preserves small but fidelity-sensitive details: `Buckling` in the
  telephone verse, `physically uncomprised`, Heisenberg's
  `indeterminism`, the `1 or 0` abstraction, the six bullet statements, and the
  distinction between brain/physical and mind/metaphysical.
- The former reader-facing description `书目信息与短引文` has been removed
  from the `No More Secondhand God` heading. The Fuller name, seven recurring
  titles, and `synergy` are now stabilized in the shared glossary and used
  consistently on this leaf.

## Required Fixes

None.

## Residual Risks

- The source intentionally repeats ideas and shifts between prose, verse,
  aphorism, and metaphysical technical language. The translation preserves
  that structure; later copy-editing should not compress it into a summary.
- `physically uncomprised`, `Buckling`, and `1 or 0` are unusual but supported
  by the scan. They should not be silently normalized during revision.
- The large structural photograph has no independent caption. Repeated title
  text on the photographed book covers does not constitute omitted prose.
