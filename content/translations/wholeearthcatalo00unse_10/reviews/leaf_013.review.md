# Leaf 013 Review

## Conclusion

accepted

## Reasons

- The three source records remain distinct. `The Ghost in the Machine` retains
  its Catalog review, bibliographic/order block, `ESCAPE FROM SPECIALIZATION`
  and `DRAW BACK TO LEAP` excerpts, Figure 10, and the complete continuation
  about the evolution of ideas. Its cover subtitle is now retained, and the
  opening taxonomy correctly treats vertebrates as a subset of chordates
  rather than equating the two. `The Year 2000` and `The Futurist` are not
  mixed into that material.
- `The Year 2000` retains the review, the complete computer-capacity excerpt,
  author/year/page/price/address information, the Whole Earth Catalog ordering
  option, the cover subtitle and deck, Daniel Bell's introduction credit, and
  all fifteen numbered items in Table IX.
- Table IX preserves its title and all of the source contrasts, including
  tertiary/quaternary versus primary/secondary activity, public-sector and
  social-account comparison, `consentives` versus `marketives`, `cybernation`,
  doubling time, and the final sensate/secular/humanist criteria.
- Figure 10 preserves the Z-Z9 zygote sequence, A-A9 adult sequence, ontogeny
  and phylogeny lines, the branching from unfinished development, the
  `protozoa` and `metazoa` labels, the Garstang credit, and the relationship
  explained in the adjacent prose.
- Prices and practical information are correctly assigned: `$6.95` postpaid
  for Koestler, `$9.95` postpaid for Kahn and Wiener, `$5 for one year` for
  `The Futurist`, both Macmillan order blocks, the World Future Society
  address, and both Whole Earth Catalog alternatives where printed.
- `The Futurist` now retains the two cover topics that can be stably recovered
  at 600 dpi, while limiting its uncertainty note to the intervening smaller
  line that the scan and OCR do not jointly establish.
- Direct comparison with the official OCR and a new 600-dpi rendering of PDF
  page 14 found no substantive omission, prose reduced to summary, invented
  background fact, or cross-entry boundary error.

## Residual Risks

- `neoteny`, `paedomorphosis`, and `gerontomorphosis` are translated
  consistently within the leaf and retain English anchors, but their proposed
  Chinese forms are not yet represented in the shared glossary.
- `consentives` and `marketives` are deliberate source coinages. The draft's
  explanatory Chinese renderings remain recoverable because the quoted English
  forms are preserved, but issue-wide wording should be settled by the
  orchestrator.
- The middle topical line on the `FUTURIST` cover remains less stable than the
  two lines identified above and should not be guessed if a second scan check
  cannot confirm it.
