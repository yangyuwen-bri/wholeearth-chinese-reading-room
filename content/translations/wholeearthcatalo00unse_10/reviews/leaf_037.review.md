accepted

## Reasons

- The 600 dpi rendering of PDF page 38 (`n37`) confirms two independent
  records: `Electricity From the Wind` in the upper half and `Coot` in the
  lower half. The translation preserves that boundary and correctly attaches
  each photograph, feature block, specification list, testimonial, price, and
  supplier address to its own product.
- The wind-generator record retains the Catalog's evaluation and question,
  `$365.00 F.O.B. Sioux City, Iowa`, the complete Dyna Technology address, all
  three illustrated feature blocks, both governor-state captions, and the
  complete specification and monthly-output values. The source's distinct
  `14` approximate maximum amps and `15` amperes at a 23-mph wind are not
  incorrectly merged.
- The wind specification tuple agrees with the scan: `10 ft`, `2 blade`,
  `6 ft`, wood, direct drive, `7 1/2 in`, `4 pole`, `200 W`, `14 A`, `15 V`,
  `270/900 RPM`, `22 in Air-Brake`, `20/26/30 kWh`, `230 A.H.`, six battery
  cells, and `2.5 V` per cell. The `Normal` and `Governing` captions, 7-mph
  charging threshold, 23-mph performance statement, and heavier-load claim
  are all present.
- The large promotional-page copy is retained, including maximum power from
  the free wind, heavy-duty 12 volt, the 12-volt wind-electric battery charger,
  and model `1222H`. The much smaller reproduced paragraphs remain unstable
  because of the source halftone and are explicitly disclosed outside the
  reader-facing translation rather than guessed.
- The Coot editorial review, Dennis McCosker testimony, Bob Grey report, John
  O. Stevans credit, advertising headline, dealer-information block, and
  `$1,695.00` price remain separately attributed. No testimonial has been
  converted into an unqualified Catalog fact.
- Every visible Coot specification agrees with the 600 dpi scan: drivetrain,
  suspension, body, frame, seat, `885-15` tires, four tire-pressure values,
  ground pressure, `27.66 C.I.D.`, `20/6 mph`, `75%`, all fluid capacities,
  fuel, torque converter, transmission and P.T.O., `18:1` through `164:1`,
  brakes, electrical system, `1,000/600 lb` capacities, and the four dimension
  values. In particular, engine oil is correctly `1.5 qts.`, not the OCR's
  corrupt alternative.
- The file contains the required seven top-level sections in the standard
  order. Source-pack mapping, `849` OCR words in `95` lines, null-risk
  disclosure, omission accounting, and historical-product-claim framing are
  internally consistent.

## Residual Risks

- The small body copy inside the reproduced Dyna Technology advertisement is
  not reliably recoverable character by character even at 600 dpi. Its stable
  large headings and model number are preserved; reconstructing the remaining
  lines from an external product leaflet would exceed the evidence on this
  leaf.
- The asterisks after `RPM` and `When fully charged` have no legible
  corresponding footnotes on the page. Retaining the markers without invented
  explanations is appropriate.
- `885-15` and the misspelled source label `Gound Pressure` are visibly printed
  forms. The translation correctly preserves the tire string while silently
  normalizing the obvious label typo in Chinese.
- All generator and vehicle performance, maintenance, and safety claims are
  historical Catalog, manufacturer, or user statements and should not be
  treated as present-day verification.
