# Production Batch Plan

The canonical per-leaf state remains `status.jsonl`. A batch is complete only
after full translation, high-resolution scan review, independent coverage
review, and orchestrator acceptance.

## Calibration Gate

| Batch | Leaves | Risk represented | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| calibration A | n0-n4 | cover; subscriber matrix; procedure; two dense contents/index pages | accepted |
| calibration B | n5, n19, n43, n63, n83 | five section starts; dense multi-entry catalog layouts | accepted |
| calibration C | n103, n121, n144-n147 | nomadics and learning starts; financial/store/Portola back matter; back cover poem | accepted |

Large production runs remain closed until the calibration leaves demonstrate:

- public-access, physical-leaf, printed-page, and scan anchors agree;
- distinct entries are inventoried and translated separately;
- prose, excerpts, captions, tables, prices with editorial meaning, and
  signatures are preserved rather than summarized;
- repeated order and bibliographic metadata is translated in full;
- reviews contain concrete `Source inventory`, `Translation coverage`, and
  `Permitted omissions` evidence.
